= How It Works =

**Using the Gutenberg Block Editor**

1. Edit any page or post in the WordPress block editor.
2. Click the **+** inserter and search for "Panorama" or "Virtual Tour".
3. Select the appropriate block (Image 360°, Virtual Tour, Video 360°, etc.).
4. Upload your image or video directly from the block, or select one from the Media Library.
5. Adjust width, height, auto-rotate, initial view, and other settings in the block sidebar.
6. See your changes live in the editor preview. Publish when you are ready.

**Using the Panorama Dashboard Settings**

1. Navigate to **Panorama Viewer** in your WordPress admin sidebar.
2. Click **Add New Panorama**.
3. Set a title, choose the **Panorama Type** (Image 3D, Image 360°, Video, Video 360°, Gallery, Tour 360°, or Google Street View), and upload your media.
4. Configure your viewer settings in the **Panorama Settings** metabox.
5. Click **Publish** or **Update**.
6. Copy the generated shortcode shown below the title (e.g. `[panorama id="123"]`) and paste it anywhere on your site.

**Using Elementor**

1. Open any page or post in the Elementor editor.
2. Find the **Panorama** widget group in the Elementor widget panel.
3. Drag the desired widget (Image 360°, Virtual Tour, Google Street View, etc.) into your layout.
4. Upload media and configure settings in the Elementor control panel.
5. Preview your changes live in the Elementor canvas and publish.

**Using WooCommerce**

1. Edit any WooCommerce product.
2. Scroll to the **Panorama Settings** metabox below the product description.
3. Choose the **Panorama Type** (Image or Video) and **Panorama Format** (Equirectangular, Cubemap, or Cylindrical).
4. Upload your 360° image or video.
5. Select the **Viewer Position** (Top, Bottom, Replace Product Image, 360° View Tab, or None for manual shortcode placement).
6. Update the product to publish the 360° experience.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What panorama formats and projections are supported? =

Three 360° projection formats are supported:

* **Equirectangular** — The standard 360° panorama format. Captured by dedicated 360° cameras (Insta360, Ricoh Theta, GoPro Max, Samsung Gear 360). The image should have a 2:1 width-to-height ratio (e.g. 4096×2048 or 8192×4096 pixels).
* **Cubemap (6 Faces)** — Upload 6 separate square images for Front, Back, Left, Right, Up (top), and Down (bottom) faces. Faster to load and free of pole distortion.
* **Cylindrical** — For wide panoramic photos taken with standard smartphone panorama modes (iPhone, Android). Supports custom horizontal angle (HAOV), vertical angle (VAOV), and vertical offset (vOffset) settings.

= What image and video file formats are supported? =

Panorama Viewer supports any image and video format that WordPress itself supports:

* **Images** — JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF.
* **Videos** — MP4, WebM, OGV (formats supported by HTML5 video).

For best quality, use JPEG or PNG for panoramic images. MP4 (H.264) is recommended for video.

= How do I create a 360° Virtual Tour? =

1. In the WordPress admin sidebar, click **Panorama Viewer → Add New**.
2. Under **Panorama Type**, select **Virtual Tour** (the new recommended type, in the top menu), or look for the Virtual Tour block in Gutenberg.
3. Add scenes, upload a panoramic image for each, and configure hotspots.
4. In the Free version, you can add up to 6 scenes and 6 hotspots per scene.
5. Publish and embed using the shortcode, Gutenberg block, or Elementor widget.

= How do I add a 360° view to a WooCommerce product page? =

1. Edit any WooCommerce product.
2. Scroll to the **Panorama Settings** metabox below the product editor.
3. Choose the panorama type (Image or Video).
4. Upload your 360° image (equirectangular, cubemap, or cylindrical).
5. Select where the viewer appears: top, bottom, replace the main product image, or in a dedicated 360° tab.
6. Update the product.

You can also paste the `[panorama_product_viewer]` shortcode anywhere on the product page template to control the exact placement.

= Can I use Elementor with this plugin? =

Yes. Panorama Viewer includes native Elementor widgets for every panorama type, available in the Elementor widget panel. All widgets include live preview. Available widgets: Image 360°, Image 3D, Video, Video 360°, Google Street View, Virtual Tour, Product Spot, and Saved Panorama.

= How do I embed a panorama using a shortcode? =

There are four shortcodes:

1. `[panorama id="123"]` — Embed a panorama from the Panorama dashboard.
2. `[virtual-tour id="456"]` — Embed a Virtual Tour.
3. `[product_spot id="789"]` — Embed a Product Spot.
4. `[panorama_product_viewer]` — Embed the 360° product panorama on a WooCommerce product page.

Replace the ID number with your actual panorama post ID, visible below the title when editing.

= How do I find the Google Street View Panorama ID? =

1. Open Google Maps in your browser.
2. Navigate to the Street View location you want to embed.
3. Enter Street View mode (click the yellow Pegman icon or click a blue Street View line).
4. Look at the browser address bar. The Panorama ID is the long alphanumeric string after `1s` in the URL — for example: `JmSoPsBPhqWvaBmOqfFzgA`.
5. Paste this ID into the **Panorama ID** field in the Google Street View block or panorama settings.

= What is the recommended image resolution for equirectangular panoramas? =

For the best viewing experience, use at least **4096×2048 pixels** (4K). For highest quality, use **8192×4096 pixels** (8K). The image must have a 2:1 width-to-height ratio.

Pro users can enable **Original Resolution Images** to bypass WordPress's 2560px image resizing, so very large panoramas display at full uploaded quality.

Yes. All panorama types are fully responsive and work on mobile devices and tablets. The **Device Orientation (Gyroscope)** feature allows mobile users to explore 360° panoramas naturally by physically moving and tilting their device.

2. Virtual Tour — multi-scene tour with scene list panel, hotspots, and compass overlay.
3. Gutenberg block editor — Virtual Tour block with live 360° preview and visual drag-and-drop hotspot placement.
4. Gutenberg block editor — 8 available Panorama blocks in the block inserter.
5. Panorama Settings metabox — panorama type selector, format options, and viewer configuration.
6. Analytics Dashboard — viewer impressions, hotspot clicks, engagement time, and 7-day activity chart.
7. Elementor widget — Image 360° widget with live preview in the Elementor editor.
8. WooCommerce product page — 360° panorama embedded in a product page with auto-rotate.
9. WooCommerce Panorama Settings — 360° product viewer configuration metabox on the product edit screen.
10. Frontend Gallery Viewer — responsive multi-panorama grid gallery with Load More pagination.

== Changelog ==

= 1.9.0 - 17 Nov, 2026 =
* **New:** Native Elementor widgets for all panorama types — 360° Image, 3D Image, Video, 360° Video, Google Street View, Virtual Tour, Product Spot, and Saved Panorama — with live preview in the Elementor editor.
* **New:** Live preview in the Panorama Settings metabox and the WooCommerce product panorama settings — changes update the viewer instantly without navigating away.
* **New:** Volume control for the Panorama Video type: a volume button and slider in the player, and a Default Volume setting for the initial playback volume.
* **New:** "Today" date range in the Analytics Dashboard, with an hourly activity breakdown.
* **Improved:** Block settings now show contextual help for each field in a "?" tooltip, consistent with the panorama settings.
* **Improved:** Panorama Video player: the control bar is positioned at the bottom of the video and the progress bar uses the full available width.
* **Improved:** Virtual Tour fullscreen: the scene list panel, hotspot popups, and compass remain on screen in fullscreen mode.
* **Improved:** The Virtual Tour hotspot editor now lists scenes by their titles instead of raw IDs.
* **Improved:** A panorama with no image or video configured shows a helpful note to editors instead of an empty black viewer.
* **Enhancement:** Added Black Friday promotional banner and campaign notices.
* **Fixed:** Dragging a new Virtual Tour hotspot in the block editor — the "+" pin now stays exactly under the cursor.
* **Fixed:** Google Street View shortcode caused a fatal error on PHP 7.1 and 7.2.
* **Fixed:** Gallery "Load More" could open the wrong item, and the popup close button could appear outside the viewer boundary.
* **Fixed:** Some Google Street View and Video options were missing from the block editor and panorama settings.
* **Fixed:** Video 360° player controls remained hidden when Autoplay was enabled.
* **Fixed:** A panorama in the Trash was incorrectly shown by its shortcode to logged-in administrators.
* **Fixed:** The Upgrade to Pro button in the panorama settings navigated to a page that does not exist.
* **Security:** Hardened viewer HTML output, embed script, analytics tracking endpoint, and CSV export.
